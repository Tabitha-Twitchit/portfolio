Loli

Loli is an ongoing performative project exploring the gap between fantasy identity and embodied experience. Loli began as a durational performance where the artist presented as Loli everyday for the period of one month. Subsequent projects have investigated fashion as social armor, LARP, and the impact of netspace on imaginative self.

Loli is the subject of an essay, 4chan Saves Lives published in 2013 by Social Malpractice Publishing.
